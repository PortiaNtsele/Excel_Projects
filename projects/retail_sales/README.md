📊 Retail Sales Analysis

🔍 Project Overview  
This project analyzes retail sales data (2009–2011) to uncover key revenue trends, customer behavior, and country-level performance. The goal is to identify insights that support better business decision-making and sales strategy optimization.

---

🎯 Objectives  
- Analyse total revenue, orders, and quantity sold  
- Identify monthly and seasonal sales trends  
- Evaluate country-level performance  
- Understand customer purchasing behavior through AOV  
- Provide actionable business recommendations  

---

🛠 Tools Used  
- Microsoft Excel  
- Power Query  
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts  
- Data Visualization  

---

📊 Dataset  
The analysis is based on retail transaction data containing sales records across multiple countries and time periods. The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Excel Power Query before analysis.

---

📈 Key Insights  
Revenue is heavily concentrated in the United Kingdom, indicating strong reliance on a single market. Sales show clear seasonality, with peaks in March and December, suggesting predictable demand cycles. Customer purchasing behavior remains stable, reflected in a consistent average order value. International markets contribute smaller but potentially scalable revenue streams.

---

💡 Recommendations  
Focus should be placed on diversifying revenue beyond the United Kingdom to reduce dependency risk. Marketing and inventory strategies should align with seasonal peaks to maximize profitability. Additionally, targeted growth strategies should be applied to underperforming international markets to improve overall revenue distribution.
